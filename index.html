How It Works

Our 4-Step Process

01 Day 1

Share Your Plans

Send your CAD drawings, BIM/Revit models, sketches, or mood boards. We evaluate the geometry and confirm deliverables immediately.

02 Day 3–5

Concept Preview

We deliver an initial lighting, camera composition, and materiality draft for your review to align creative direction.

03 Day 5–7

One Revision Round

Fine-tune colors, lighting temperatures, landscaping, or fixtures based on client comments. One dedicated refinement round is included free.

04 Day 7–10

Final Delivery

Production 4K Ultra-HD renders and high-bitrate video files delivered securely via Google Drive, fully ready for pitches and marketing.
